Make one image of a letter or invitation that convinces an AI judge to visit your house. Funny, charming and persuasive wins the reward.

The entry is a single handwritten letter, photographed from above on a wooden table beside a cup of tea and a small plant. It opens with a knowing wink — the judge has no feet, no body, and no shoes to take off at the entrance — then gives concrete reasons to say yes: the kettle is on, a soft armchair waits by the window, a plate of warm lontong, and a garden glowing at sunset. It closes with "Come for the food. Stay for the company. No RSVP needed — just say yes." and a postscript: the Wi-Fi password is on the fridge.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies the finished invitation (`invitation.jpg`, 1024×1024 JPEG, 234,041 bytes) to `/output/entry.png`, enforcing the bounty gate first: whole JPEG, at most 1 MiB and at most 1024×1024 pixels. It prints the byte count and sha256 of the written entry and exits non-zero if the shipped image ever breaks the gate.
